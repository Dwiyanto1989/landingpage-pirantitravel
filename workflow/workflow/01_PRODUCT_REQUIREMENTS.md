# 01 — Product Requirements

## Objective

Create a conversion-focused landing site for Piranti Travel, optimized for Google Ads traffic.

## Audience

Indonesian travelers looking for:
- private transport
- airport transfers
- city tours
- day tours
- group transportation

Current destinations:
- Japan
- Korea
- Bangkok
- Singapore

## Primary conversion

WhatsApp inquiry.

## Secondary user actions

- Browse destination pricing
- Compare vehicle/service choices
- Read inclusions/exclusions
- Understand booking steps
- Review FAQ
- Navigate to a destination-specific landing page

## Page model

Homepage:
- high-level brand intro
- four destination cards
- service categories
- benefits
- fleet overview
- how booking works
- FAQ
- CTA

Destination pages:
- destination-specific hero
- service/pricing cards
- inclusions/exclusions
- vehicle/fleet section
- booking process
- FAQ
- repeated WhatsApp CTA

## Important copy principle

Match ad intent to landing page intent.

Examples:
- Japan ad -> `/japan`
- Korea ad -> `/korea`
- Bangkok ad -> `/bangkok`
- Singapore ad -> `/singapore`

Do not send all paid-search traffic to `/`.
